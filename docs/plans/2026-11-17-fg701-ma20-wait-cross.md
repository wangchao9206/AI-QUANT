# FG701 MA20: wait for a fresh cross after every close

User revision: do not reverse upon closing. Reenter only after a new downward/upward crossing of MA20. MA20 remains the user-confirmed 20 one-minute closes, not 20 daily closes. Preserve no profit target, 10-tick stop, existing 3-tick held-position opposite-mean exit, one lot, same dates and costs.

## Frozen execution rules

- Reuse independently validated continuous one-minute SMA20 with the original 19 observed warmup candles. No indicator daily reset or future data.
- Keep each day's initial close-side entry as before, confirmed at minute close and filled next observed open. The user changes reentry after a close, not the first daily entry.
- Every exit (mean exit, stop, opening gap stop) leaves the strategy flat. No opposite fill at the exit timestamp and no reentry in that minute.
- The completed close of the exit minute initializes a new close-minus-MA20 anchor. Do not reuse the crossing that caused the exit, or infer a fresh crossing before/after an intraminute stop.
- On a later completed traded minute: previous eligible close-minus-MA20 <=0 and current >0 schedules a long; previous >=0 and current <0 schedules a short. Equality alone never schedules entry. Compare each close to its own contemporaneous MA20. Execute at the next observed open. Remaining above/below does not satisfy a new-cross condition.
- Reentry may be either direction, according to the fresh crossing. A reopened position again has a 10-tick fixed stop and no take-profit.
- Held-position opposite close >=3 ticks across MA20 schedules close next observed open. Stops act intraminute, gap stops at observed open. Close never triggers a new position.
- No entries/reentries at/after 14:58, liquidate at 14:59 open. Position/wait state resets per trading day; indicator does not reset. Day-end liquidation has priority and cannot reenter.
- O-H-L-C and O-L-H-C are independently replayed for consistency. With one fixed stop, no target and no same-minute reentry, intraminute stop ordering cannot change fills; verify that equivalence rather than assume it. Every fill keeps separate assumed fees and adverse slippage.
- Preserve all old scripts/results and write a separate study and chart.
